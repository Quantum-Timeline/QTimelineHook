How claims work

What you can do with a claim

  1. Hold the position you traded

    A buy leaves you with the claim you chose plus the paired claim on your input collateral.

  2. Merge before the result

    One YES and one NO of the same asset merge back into that asset, any time before resolution.

  3. Redeem a winner 1 : 1

    After a valid result, the winning claim redeems for its collateral. The losing claim is worth zero.

  4. Cancelled pays half

    If no result arrives within 48 hours of the scheduled report time, anyone can cancel; every claim redeems for 50%.
