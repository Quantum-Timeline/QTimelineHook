How voting works

From deposit to the next market

  1. Deposit QTL

    Escrow the amount you want counted before voting opens. Moving tokens between wallets later adds no weight.

  2. Vote once

    Pick one of two fixed thresholds for the next event. Each wallet votes a single time.

  3. Anyone finalizes

    After the window closes anyone can finalize. The most votes win; a tie or zero votes picks candidate B.

  4. Withdraw your escrow

    Your tokens come back once voting ends, or before it starts. The winner binds the second market's configuration.
