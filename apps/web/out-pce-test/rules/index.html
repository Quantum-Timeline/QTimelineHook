Vault · split

One deposit, two claims

Deposit ETH or USDG into a market vault to mint equal YES and NO outcome tokens for that collateral.

Pool · trade

Three independent pools

ETH-YES and ETH-NO expose conditional asset prices. YES-NO provides an implied outcome price. LP fee is 0.30%; QTimeline adds no protocol fee.

Oracle · resolve

BEA release, hybrid authorization

A project bot reads the first official BEA release and reports the printed month-over-month Core PCE in basis points. The report digest requires both ECDSA and ML-DSA-44 signatures. These keys share project control; the signature does not independently prove the BEA number. The release and its hash are public.

Claim · redeem

Winning side pays 1:1

After a valid result, winning claims redeem for their collateral. If unresolved 48 hours after the scheduled report time, anyone can trigger cancellation; each individual claim redeems for half, while a matched YES/NO pair merges for full collateral.

Risk: pool prices move with liquidity and may diverge from any fair value. USDG depends on issuer transfer policy. Smart contract, oracle parsing, signer, RPC and hosting risks remain. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
