14. Trust Model:
    - Core contracts are upgradeable proxies. The deployer wallet owns every ProxyAdmin and beacon with no timelock, so it can replace logic, including the vault logic that holds collateral. The upgrades of 24 Nov 2026 used that power.
    - The deployer binds the data source of each market. The resolver refuses to sign when the on-chain binding does not match its source.
    - The resolver ECDSA key and ML-DSA-44 key are controlled by the project. The dual signature protects against the loss of one key, not against the operator.
    - Collateral per market is capped at 0.1 ETH and 500 USDG by the factory.
    - Opening liquidity is thin (28 USDG and about 0.0045 ETH). The treasury is the counterparty to trades in the YES / NO pool. In the ETH pools only small trades (up to about 5 USDG) quote.
